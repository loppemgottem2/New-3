Character

During a match your shirt color is your secret codename color, so nobody knows who is who.

How to play

Bystanders must survive. One of them secretly has a revolver. Walk over the glowing gun parts — collect 5 to build your own revolver (only one built revolver can exist at a time).

The Murderer has a knife. Kill everyone without getting caught. You can also throw the knife — then go pick it up.

Whoever holds the revolver is the Sheriff. If the Sheriff shoots an innocent bystander, the Sheriff dies too and the gun drops. Anyone except the murderer can pick a dropped gun up. Chain jumps and air-strafe to build speed, and shove the furniture around for cover.

Everybody gets a random codename and color each round, so nobody knows who is who. Watch who carries a knife, and who stands over the bodies…

Phone controls

Left thumb: move (push to the edge to sprint). Right thumb: drag to look. Buttons: jump, draw/hide weapon, stab/shoot (drag on it to aim too), throw, pinch with two fingers to zoom the camera in/out, ⚙ settings.

Computer controls

WASD move · Mouse look (click to lock) · Shift sprint · Space jump · Left click attack · Q draw/hide · Right click / G throw knife · Mouse wheel zoom / V third person · Tab scoreboard · F next spectate · Esc / P settings

The X button

In a match: X = give up and spectate (you die). Press X again = back to the main menu. X on the main menu = leave the game.
