How to play

Props

Tap furniture to turn into it, stay still, climb walls by jumping into them, and survive until the timer ends.

Seekers

Wait out the black screen, then find every prop with your knife, crowbar and revolver, but each hit on real furniture costs 10 health.

Camera

Shift lock aims with the crosshair, and without it you aim wherever you tap or click.

Controls

Move with the joystick or WASD, look by dragging or with the mouse, and pinch or scroll to zoom.

After you die

Fly around freely, use the arrow to watch players, or press X for the main menu.
