How to play

Every round the players are split into two teams.

Props spawn as stiff, T-posing people. During the hiding time, find a good spot and tap / click any piece of furniture to turn into it (tap another piece to change). Then keep still: furniture that moves around gets noticed!

Shift lock (🔒 button or L): ON = you always face where the camera looks and aim with the crosshair in the middle. OFF = the camera moves freely around you, you face where you walk, and you aim / shoot wherever you tap or click on the screen.

Seekers are blindfolded while the props hide. Then they spawn with a knife and a revolver in their hotbar. Find the props and take them out — but hitting real furniture costs you health.

Seekers win when every prop is found. Props win if at least one survives until the time runs out (or every seeker is down).

Phone controls

Left thumb: move (push to the edge to sprint). Right thumb: drag to look. Props: tap furniture on the screen to turn into it. Seekers: tap a hotbar slot to hold that item, ✕ puts it away, 🎒 opens the inventory. With shift lock off, tap the screen to stab / shoot that spot; STAB/SHOOT attacks where the camera points. THROW throws the knife. Pinch with two fingers to zoom the camera, ⚙ settings.

Computer controls

WASD move · Mouse look (click to lock) · Shift sprint · Space jump · Left click attack the spot under the cursor (props: click furniture to become it) · Right-drag turn the camera · L shift lock (mouse look + crosshair) · 1–5 hotbar · Q put item away · I inventory · G throw knife (right click in shift lock) · Mouse wheel zoom · Tab scoreboard · F next spectate · Esc / P settings

The X button

In a match: X = give up and spectate (you die). Press X again = back to the main menu. X on the main menu = leave the game.
